## 👋 About me

I'm a **Mechatronics Engineer** and **Master's student in Electronic Engineering (RF)** at the **University of Algarve**, Portugal. My work sits where hardware, firmware and communications meet, from the schematic and the PCB to the code, the radio link and the field test.

I started in electronics and product development in Brazil. I then worked as a **Field Applications Engineer** supporting R&D teams on microcontrollers and components. Today I'm back in **R&D**, building connected systems.

## 🔬 What I'm working on

- 💧 **IoT for water monitoring and smart cities.** I'm an R&D Engineer at **Smartconnect**, working on telemetry, sensing and remote supervision for water management and smart irrigation.
- 🛰️ **Nanosatellite research.** My Master's research at the University of Algarve is on nanosatellite development and the RF links that go with it.
- 📡 **Connectivity.** LoRa, NB-IoT, MQTT and OTA firmware updates for connected devices.
- 🔧 **Embedded firmware.** Zephyr RTOS, LVGL and C/C++ on ESP32, STM32 and Toshiba microcontrollers.
