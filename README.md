## 👋 About me

Master's student in **Electronic Engineering (RF)** and **Mechatronics Engineer** with a background in **R&D**, **FAE** and **firmware**. I like projects that cross the whole stack: board design, low-level code, radio links and the tools to test them.

- 🛰️ Building a **nanosatellite** as part of my Master's at the University of Algarve
- 🔧 Firmware on **Zephyr RTOS**, LVGL and bare-metal MCUs
- 📡 IoT connectivity: LoRa, NB-IoT, MQTT and OTA updates
- 🧪 FPGA experiments in **VHDL** on the Terasic DE1-SoC
